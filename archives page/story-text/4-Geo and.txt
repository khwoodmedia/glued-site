2. Geo and the Hyphy Harp
The past three months for Geo have consisted of backyard shows, concerts, All-Star Weekend, studio sessions, 16-hour drives, national anthems, and countless fangirl moments. Signing up for LLCs and toting her harp across festival stages in the Los Angeles heat. Joining sold-out sets and sorting through endless DMs, all while her supporters grow.
In the middle of January, Geo showed up for a soundcheck and audition, but she didn’t know that. After passing the first of unannounced auditions, she was invited to play the concert. I remember that first show, Twista was performing and Geo kept looking to Harry for the keys to different songs. Harry didn’t look especially sure either. Still, the harp added something elegant and ethereal to the set, something that couldn’t be replicated. From that moment on, she was in.
She was quickly inducted into the legendary Yee Section. What started as quiet glances of reassurance while performing turned into effortless stage presence within weeks. The Bay Area music community was catching on too, artists, labels, and creatives alike were paying close attention to what Geo was creating.
By the time this magazine comes out, she’ll be at 20k followers on Instagram. Since meeting Geo, we’ve had countless conversations where she’ll exclaim, “I’m just being myself! I’ve never felt more myself!” a response to the continuous wave of opportunities that keep coming. The best part is knowing that the world is discovering and falling in love with someone who shows up as their full, authentic self.
Through meeting her friends and family, you feel the synergy among the people closest to her. She once told me how much she loved mixing friend groups, how they all got along. I (resident pessimist) doubted this. But after meeting her friends from all parts of her life, I got it. Geo has a spirit that attracts people who are very “come as you are.” And her greatest gift is her ability to share her talent with the world and reflect what’s possible when you show up fully as yourself.
