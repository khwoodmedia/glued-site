Story 7: Creating Kehlani’s Record Store Inspired Pop-Up for Her New Album “Crash”
It was 2:00 AM on a Tuesday night in June when Chalberto’s phone buzzed. David Ali, Kehlani’s manager, was on the line with an urgent request. Kehlani had a pop-up shop in Oakland the following Friday, and Chalberto was tasked with creating an old 80s/90s record store infused with her unique style for her new album release, “Crash.”
Excited and inspired, Chalberto immediately began sketching ideas and getting them approved by David Ali. The color palette was dark royal blue and black, and accents included window decals and wall art. The design featured album covers of greats like Lauryn Hill, Musiq Soulchild, Amerie, and other soulful classics. He quickly pulled together a team and shopped for supplies, including vinyl, ink, plywood, epoxy, and new tools.
His vision included window decals, floor details, custom wood designs, and framing canvases.
For three days, Chalberto and his team worked nonstop, posting Instagram stories of their progress throughout the night. They printed and framed canvases with Kehlani’s intergalactic visuals, wrapped walls, painted tables, installed acrylic shelves lined with lights, and created the iconic blue and black “Crash” tables for autographs. When the day of the pop-up arrived, Kehlani and her team were amazed by the transformation. The space perfectly the mix of a nostalgic record store and Kehlani’s new age visuals. Chalberto and his team managed to execute have every meticulously crafted.
